# Services & Product Catalog

**Purs & Gold Limited · Master price book v2.0 · 9 September 2026 · Owner decisions applied · Naira for Nigeria, US dollars for Lusophone markets, exclusive of taxes**

This catalog replaces the June 2025 Service Catalogue and every price table inside the February 2026 proposals. Each item carries a code, the scope it covers, the price, and a status. Confirmed items may be quoted today. Proposed items may be quoted once the owner approves them. Decision items may not be quoted until priced.

Four rules from the owner's decisions run through every table:

- **2026 price review.** Every figure carried from the June 2025 catalogue is uplifted by 25% and rounded (to the nearest ₦500 below ₦20,000, ₦2,500 up to ₦100,000, ₦5,000 above). These rows show the 2025 figure in the note. Approved.
- **Billing is monthly.** All subscriptions and retainers are billed monthly in advance with no yearly commitment required.
- **Optional annual prepay.** A client who chooses to pay 12 months upfront receives 10% off the subscription fees for that year. Onboarding fees are never discounted. The choice is the client's; it is never a condition.
- **No unpriced item.** Any product or service without a set price is quoted on the client's business needs. "Quote-based" rows are therefore sellable today; they are not waiting on a decision.

Tier vocabulary is the same everywhere: **Starter**, **Growth**, **Scale**, and **Enterprise** for custom scope.

Sections C and D are backed by the company's own products, described in the Software Product Portfolio. Products are sold as a single deployment per client plus a subscription: a one-time deployment and onboarding fee from the ladder, then a monthly fee for the client's own environment. Where no product exists yet (hospital, hotel), the ladder is a quote-based implementation of a third-party platform.
